Invited talk delivered in **Session 1: Quantum Materials and Devices** at [ISSMD-2026](https://sites.google.com/iitrpr.ac.in/issmd2026/home), hosted by IIT Ropar on 23–25 October 2026.

The talk covered two connected threads from our group:

1. **Interpretable ML screening for nuclear-spin-quiet qubit hosts** (work by [Md Mahshook A](/author/md-mahshook-a/), published in *Physical Review Materials*): using composition-only models and Rashomon-set agreement across ~45,000 stable compounds to identify 122 candidate host materials, including layered chalcogenides, alkaline-earth tellurides, high-ε oxides, and ternaries.
2. **The ¹⁷O hyperfine bath of Ce³⁺ polarons in CeO₂** (work by Chithresh T M): showing how orbital orientation of the localised 4f¹ state selects a single strongly coupled first-shell ¹⁷O neighbour (2.02 MHz by DFT), yielding an exceptionally quiet spin bath with testable ESEEM/HYSCORE signatures.
